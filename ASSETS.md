The facility, mission patch and crew are fictional: no logos, agency insignia or real brand names. The rocket is the real Saturn V (the model above): it carries no agency insignia, and its "USA" and "UNITED STATES" lettering and US flags stay because its licence allows no changes. The earlier Mark III suit had its NASA logo left out, as NASA's media guidelines ask.
